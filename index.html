1. The short version

  • Your account, bets, bankroll, unit size, bet slip, and sportsbook notes are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. We do not currently operate servers that receive this data.
  • Accounts are stored on this device only for now. Your password is never stored in plain text; only a salted, one-way hash is kept on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising.
  • To show odds and team logos, your device connects directly to third-party services (The Odds API and ESPN's image servers), which can see your IP address and standard request information.

2. Information we process

Information you enter (stored locally on your device):

  • Account details: email address, optional display name, a salted password hash (never the password itself), and the date/time and version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted, plus your age confirmation.
  • Betting records: bets and parlays you log (date, sport, event, bet type, odds, stake, units, result, sportsbook name, notes), your bankroll and unit-size settings, and your current bet slip.
  • Linked sportsbook notes: a sportsbook name, the username/email you use there, and optional notes. Do not enter sportsbook passwords. Browser local storage is not encrypted, so the Service no longer offers a password field for sportsbooks. If an older version saved something in that field, the app flags it so you can delete it.

Information collected automatically by third parties when the app loads data: When your device requests odds or logos, the providers below receive your IP address, browser/device type (user-agent), the time of the request, and the requested resource (for example, which sport's odds). We do not use cookies, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or precise geolocation.

Sensitive personal information: We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by state law (such as government IDs, financial account numbers, precise geolocation, or health data). Your bet history can reveal personal habits, which is one reason it stays on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: show odds, build your bet slip, track your bets and units, and calculate profit/loss, win rate, and ROI.
  • To create and secure your local account, verify your login, and record your acceptance of these terms and your age confirmation.
  • To remember your preferences (such as unit size and the first-launch notice).

We do not use your information for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not use it to train AI models.

4. Third-party services

  • The Odds API (the-odds-api.com) supplies odds and the list of sports. Your device sends requests directly to it.
  • ESPN's content delivery network (a.espncdn.com) serves team logos. Your device loads the images directly from it.
  • Sportsbooks: sportsbook names (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, and others) are shown for comparison only. We do not send them your data.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. We have no control over their practices.

5. Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising

We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not process it for targeted advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months. Because of this, there is nothing to opt out of. If that changes, we will update this policy first and provide an opt-out, including honoring Global Privacy Control (GPC) and other universal opt-out signals where the law requires.
